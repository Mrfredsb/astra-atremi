Engineered for Absolute Control

To deliver flawless data models and ensure total bio-containment, our laboratory facility has been engineered around rigorous, non-negotiable physical and structural specifications.

High-Capacity Power Distribution

Our operational deployment areas are equipped with a dedicated, heavy-duty 3-phase power supply. This framework is structurally critical to sustain the stable, continuous high-capacity electrical draws required by our commercial HVAC setups and automated diagnostics instrumentation arrays.

UKAS-Compliant Fluid Management

Advanced, high-flow mains water connections feed directly into our UKAS-compliant multi-stage reverse osmosis purification networks. Crucially, all operational trade effluent discharges entirely into local mains foul sewer systems to handle specialised fluid throughput safely.

Environmental Isolation & Access Control

Maintaining a perfect baseline requires uncompromised security. Our perimeters utilise high-level steel door upgrades and advanced biometric access points. These frameworks serve a dual structural mandate: delivering robust physical asset isolation and providing air-tight micro-climate environmental sealing.
